Part 2 · How a topic works

Every topic is a deck of scenes

You are inside one right now.

Move around
  • → or Space: next scene
  • ←: previous scene
  • M or the Scenes button: jump to any scene
  • The bar at the bottom shows your progress

Part 3 · Study and get help

How to study one topic

A simple routine beats a long session.

1 · Read the story

Get the big idea before the syntax.

2 · Run every cell

Press Run, then change the code and run again.

3 · Break it on purpose

Cause the common mistakes and read the errors.

4 · Do the practice arena

Do Easy to Crazy without peeking at solutions.

5 · Do the take-home

Use your own laptop and your own words.

6 · Teach it back

Explain it in two sentences to someone, or write it down.
